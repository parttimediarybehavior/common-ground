# Prototype engine contract

ES module `./engine.js`, browser and Node, no dependencies.

Exports `createWorld(seed = 42, options = {})`, `stepWorld(world)` (mutates, returns world), `advance(world, days)` (mutates, 0–1,000 days per call), `applyIntervention(world, type, details = {})`, `cloneWorld(world)` (deep clone), `summarize(world)`, `findPath(world, from, to)` (list of {x,y}, excluding origin; null if impossible).

World is plain JSON: `{version:1, seed, day, width:24, height:16, cells, agents, settlements, bridge, drought, events, ledger, options}`. Cells: `{x,y,terrain:'land'|'water',resource:null|'food'|'wood',stock:number,capacity:number}`; bridge water cells are passable only if `bridge.open`.

Bridge `{x:11,y:8,open:true,repair:0,required:18}` spans x=11 and x=12 at y=8. River x=11/12. Settlements `{id:0|1,name,x,y,food:number}`. Agents `{id,name,x,y,home:0|1,food,wood,health,alive,action,reason,target:null|{x,y},contributions,received,helped:object,memories:array}`; no fixed personality labels. `action` describes latest action; `reason` explains actual local decision. Memories `{day,eventId,text}` cite existing events. Events `{id,day,type,text,actor:null|id,target:null|id,location:null|{x,y},evidence:object}`. Ledger `{initialFood,produced,consumed,lost,initialWood,woodProduced,woodUsed}` tracks conservation. Drought `{until:number,side:0}` or null.

Options `{memory:true,sharing:true,travelSteps:2,knowledge:'oracle'}`; knowledge alternatives `local` and `reports`. Keyed randomness ensures deterministic replay and prevents intervention-induced random stream shifts. Agents also have `known` resource observations keyed by row-major cell index, `seen` cell observation dates, innate `willingness`/`gatherRate`, and `missedMeals`. A known record is `{x,y,resource,stock,seenAt,order,source,via}`. Source is the original observer's ID; via is the immediate forwarder ID or null. Local observation radius is Manhattan distance two; refresh occurs in a morning pass and immediately before each action. Forecasts do not read unseen current stocks.

World also includes `statistics:{travel,foodGathered,emptyArrivals,reports,harvests,observations}` and `blockedReports:[]`. Observation order is monotonically increasing across same-day observations, so newer depletion can supersede morning stocks. Identical-value same-day reports are not transmitted. Failed harvests, chosen destinations, reports and blocked reports are recorded. Carrying food is capped at fourteen food units.

`summarize`: `{day,alive,population,food,pantry,meanHealth,gifts,contributions,withdrawals,bridgeOpen,bridgeRepair,foodBalanceError,woodBalanceError}`. food includes private+pantry+resource stock; wood includes private+resource stock (deposited repair wood counted as used). Round UI display, not simulation state.

Summary also includes cumulative `travelSteps`, `totalFoodGathered`, `emptyArrivals`, `reports`. Cumulative travel differs from the `options.travelSteps` movement allowance. The displayed statistics do not infer utility, intelligence or social coordination.

Interventions: `drought` reduces west-bank food growth for 45 days; `bridge` closes bridge and resets repair progress (wood spent on prior construction remains used); `forget` clears remembered help and sets memory false; `rain` ends drought. `local`, `reports`, `oracle` change knowledge mode. `block-report` takes `{day,actor,target}` for a future day (up to 1,000), schedules withholding that actor's one candidate transmission to that neighbor, and records both scheduling and actual withholding. If no candidate transmission occurs then none is withheld. `skip-repair` takes `{day,actor}` and omits that actor's contribution after reaching the repair shore while retaining their timber; schedule lives in optional `omittedRepairs` and both scheduling/actual omission are recorded. `forget-helper` takes `{actor,helper}`, removes only that actor's remembered association and remembered incoming gift receipts for that helper, retaining physical inventories, historical world events and everyone else's memory. Clearing a help association affects the starting record, not the ability to share later; new help memories remain possible. All interventions are recorded, never silently inserted.

`save.js` exports `validateWorld`, `validateComparison`, `serializeComparison` and shared `MAX_SAVE_BYTES` (64 MiB). Serialization validates, produces compact JSON, and refuses oversized output before download. Validation checks physical/provenance structure and counters, not cryptographic authenticity or replay of hand-edited history. Save-format version 1 is experimental; historical earlier-rule outputs may intentionally fail current structural validation.

## Selective gift omission and ordered inspection

`skip-gift` accepts `{day,actor,target}` for a future day≤1000. It omits ONLY a gift that these participants actually choose that day, after recipient/probability selection and before the debit. The donor retains food, recipient gets no transfer or help credit, and the sharing workday is spent (no substitute gathering). Ordinary eating still occurs. If the chosen gift does not occur, no omission is executed. Optional `omittedGifts` schedules and their announced/actual provenance are validated; old worlds without the field remain valid. This is a controlled workday omission, not a claim about realistic psychology.

`stepWorld(w,onAction?)` and `advance(w,days,onAction?)` can emit copied inspection views just before each living resident chooses an action: `{day,actor,phase:'before-action',agents:[{id,name,x,y,food,wood,alive}],cells:[{x,y,resource,stock}]}`. Earlier actors have already acted and eaten. The callback receives no original world references; modifying its view cannot affect replay. Without a callback, no views are constructed and ordinary simulation behavior is unchanged. Inspection callbacks should collect views without throwing. These omniscient audit views are case-building evidence; the normal mystery interface exposes only selected facts after an appropriate test, not all private state.
