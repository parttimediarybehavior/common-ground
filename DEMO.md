# One conversation, two routes

Open `standalone.html` directly in a browser. No server, account, model credits or network access is needed to play. The default is seed 19, "One conversation".

Both worlds start on day 25 with the same food, inhabitants, drought and ability to exchange observations. Only the right-hand world schedules withholding Eli's report to Orin on day 30. Orin is already selected; the journal shows his events.

1. Step five days to day 30. Eli reports that patch (5,4) holds approximately **0.38 food**. That patch is five tiles from Orin, outside his two-tile sight. Without that message, Orin's older observation predicts roughly **6.84**. The right journal explicitly records the withheld report.
2. Step to day 31. The route changes: with the report Orin reaches (6,6); without it he reaches (4,4). Both had initially moved to the same (4,6) on day 30.
3. Step to day 32. With the report Orin gathers **5.31 food** at (8,6); without it he gathers **0.75** at (5,4). Expand the recorded evidence for the exact amounts, forecast and source.

The message is about a depleted patch, not a magical new resource. It changes which older belief Orin still trusts. The other branch later finds more food too; this is a change in his route and immediate harvest, not a guarantee of permanent advantage or survival. The controlled comparison suppresses that one transmission while preserving the other rules.

Other things to try:

- **Disrupted crossing:** watch inhabitants spend real timber and work to reconnect the banks during a drought. Inspect the contributors instead of assuming a leader or collective plan exists.
- **Stale maps:** compare private local observations with nearby reports. Both modes can look again immediately before acting. Follow forecasts and routes; reports need not improve every outcome.
- **Memory trial:** compare the same crisis with and without remembered food help. This can be a negative result. Erasing help memory does not erase navigation or patch observations.

Pause before inspecting a history. Save/open preserves the two full worlds. At day 1,000 the clock stops; start a new comparison to continue exploring. This is an exploratory, rule-driven toy, not a finished autonomous society or a proven game business.
